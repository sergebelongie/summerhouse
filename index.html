📍 Getting Here

Gyvelbakken 10 is located in the scenic, hilly terrain of Veddinge Bakker in Odsherred.

Driving Directions

From Copenhagen or Kalundborg, take Route 21 towards Odsherred. Turn towards Asnæs / Veddinge Bakker onto Høve Stræde / Veddingevej.

Navigating Veddinge Bakker Hills

  • Gravel Roads: Gyvelbakken is a narrow gravel road with steep inclines. Please drive slowly (max 20 km/h) to protect cars and prevent dust.
  • Passing Spots: Be mindful of oncoming cars on narrow bends; yield at wider roadside pockets.

Public Transit Options

Take the regional train to Asnæs Station. From Asnæs Station, local bus 560 or a short 8-minute taxi ride brings you directly to Veddinge Bakker.

🅿️ Parking Guide

Please follow these guidelines for parking on the property to ensure accessibility for neighbours and emergency vehicles.

On-Site Parking Capacity

  • Driveway Space: There is room for 2 to 3 cars directly on the gravel driveway of Gyvelbakken 10.
  • Tandem Parking: When hosting multiple cars (e.g. team offsites), please park close together in tandem to leave room for maneuverability.

Important Neighborhood Rules

  • No Roadside Parking: Do not park along the narrow gravel road of Gyvelbakken. The road must remain clear at all times for emergency access and local traffic.
  • Gravel Care: Drive carefully when turning into the driveway to avoid kicking up loose stones.

♻️ Waste Sorting (Odsherred Kommune)

Odsherred Municipality enforces mandatory sorting for all residential properties. Bins are located near the driveway entrance:

  • Restaffald (General Trash): Non-recyclable household items, diapers, wrapped food packaging.
  • Madaffald (Food Waste): All organic food scraps placed in the provided green biodegradable bags.
  • Plast / Papir / Metal: Clean plastic containers, cardboard, metal cans, and foil.
  • Glass & Bottles: Can be disposed of at local municipal collection points or returned at supermarkets.

🏖️ Beaches & Coastal Areas

Odsherred boasts some of Sealand’s finest coastline. Here are top beach recommendations nearby:

Veddinge Strand (15 min walk / 3 min drive)

Our local beach along Sejerø Bay. Great for quick morning dips or evening coastal walks. The shoreline varies between pebbles and soft sand pockets.

Høve Strand (5 min drive)

Family-friendly sandy beach with pine trees right down to the water, public parking, and ice cream stands during summer months.

Gudmindrup Strand (12 min drive)

Widely considered one of Denmark's best beaches. Wide white sand dunes, shallow child-friendly waters, and lifeguard towers during peak season.

Ordrup Næs (10 min drive)

Scenic natural peninsula offering dramatic walks, coastal viewpoints, and great spots for sea kayaking or fishing.

🌲 Community Rules (Strandgaardens Grundejerforening)

Gyvelbakken 10 is part of Strandgaardens Grundejerforening (www.strandgaardens.dk). Please observe local neighborhood etiquette:

  • Quiet Hours: Please keep outdoor music and loud voices down between 22:00 and 08:00 to respect nearby homeowners.
  • Speed Limit: Maximum 20 km/h on all gravel roads in Veddinge Bakker.
  • Outdoor Fires & BBQs: Open campfires are strictly forbidden in dry months due to risk of wildfire in the hills. Use only the charcoal/gas BBQ on the stone pad.
  • Dogs & Pets: Must be kept on a leash in community areas and kept off furniture.
