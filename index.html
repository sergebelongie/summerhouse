Parking Instructions

There is ample parking on the property.

  • Please park on the gravel driveway in front of the house.
  • There is space for 2-3 standard-sized vehicles.
  • Please do not park on the grass to protect the landscaping.
  • Ensure you are not blocking the road (Gyvelbakken) for neighbors or emergency vehicles.

Heating & Cooling

Heat Pump

The house is primarily heated by an air-to-air heat pump located in the living room.

  • Use the remote control provided on the wall bracket.
  • Recommended temperature: 20-22°C.
  • In summer, this unit can also be used for air conditioning (select the 'Cool' mode).

Wood Stove

You are welcome to use the wood-burning stove for extra warmth and coziness. Please only use dry, clean firewood.

Check-out Checklist

Before you leave, please help us by completing the following:

  • Empty the fridge and freezer.
  • Take out all trash to the bins outside.
  • Start the dishwasher.
  • Turn the heat pump down to 10°C (if staying in winter months).
  • Ensure all doors and windows are locked.
