Getting Here

Address: Gyvelbakken 10, Veddinge Bakker, 4550 Asnæs

Driving & Navigation

Veddinge Bakker is known for its steep, winding gravel roads. Please drive slowly (max 20 km/h) to minimize dust and protect pedestrians.

  • When using GPS, ensure it directs you via Veddingevej.
  • During winter or heavy rain, approach the final hill on Gyvelbakken with steady momentum.

Public Transport

If you are arriving from Copenhagen, taking the train is a scenic and relaxing option.

  • Train (Tog): Take the regional train from Copenhagen Central Station to Holbæk. In Holbæk, transfer to the local train (Lokaltog 510R) heading towards Nykøbing Sjælland. You can get off at either Asnæs Station or Fårevejle Station.
  • Bus: From Asnæs or Fårevejle station, you can catch Bus 568, which stops at Veddinge, Riisvej (Veddingevej). From the bus stop, it is a steep, roughly 15-minute walk up into the hills to reach the house. (Note: We highly recommend arranging a taxi from the station if you have heavy luggage).

Parking

Proper parking is essential in Veddinge Bakker to ensure emergency vehicles and neighbors can always pass on the narrow roads.

  • Driveway Capacity: There is space for 2-3 cars to park sequentially on our property.
  • Do Not Block the Road: Never leave cars parked out on the main gravel road (Gyvelbakken).
  • Visitor Parking: If you have day guests, please coordinate so their cars fit within the property line.

Waste Sorting

Odsherred Kommune has strict sorting rules. Please separate garbage into the designated bins outside the property:

  • Madaffald: Food waste ONLY in the green biodegradable bags (found under the sink).
  • Restaffald: General non-recyclable waste in standard plastic bags (securely tied).
  • Plast / Papir / Metal: Clean, dry recyclables in the dual bin. No bags; put directly into the container.
  • Glas: Glass bottles must be taken to the municipal glass containers (e.g., at the supermarket).

Supplies

We try to keep the house stocked with basics. If you use the last of something, please try to replace it or let us know.

  • Toilet Paper & Paper Towels: Backstock is located in the bottom cabinet of the bathroom.
  • Trash Bags: Found under the kitchen sink. Green bags for food waste, gray for general waste.
  • Coffee Beans: Spare bags are kept in the upper pantry cupboard.
  • Firewood: Stacked outside under the deck shelter.
  • Pantry Basics: You are welcome to use spices, oils, and dry goods. Please leave the kitchen as you found it.

Espresso Machine

Start your morning right! The machine uses whole beans.

  • Water Tank: Ensure the tank on the back is filled with fresh, cold water.
  • Grinder: Beans go in the top hopper. Do not adjust the grind size while the machine is off.
  • Steamer: Purge the steam wand before and after frothing milk, and wipe it with a damp cloth immediately.

Fireplace (Brændeovn)

Enjoying a fire is a must, but please operate the stove safely.

  • Lighting: Ensure the air damper is fully open before lighting kindling.
  • Fuel: Use ONLY the provided dry firewood. Never burn trash or treated wood.
  • Ash: DO NOT empty the ash. We will handle it between stays to prevent fire hazards.

Sauna

The indoor electric sauna takes about 30-45 minutes to heat up.

  • Controls: Use the dials on the front of the heater (Timer and Temperature).
  • Water: You may lightly ladle fresh water over the stones for steam. Do not pour saltwater.
  • Towels: Always sit on a towel to protect the wood benches.

Supermarkets

  • SuperBrugsen (Asnæs Centret): Large selection, bakery, and butcher. (7 min drive)
  • Meny (Ordrup): Great for summer specialties and wine. (10 min drive)

Beaches

Odsherred offers some of the best coastline in Denmark.

  • Veddinge Strand: The closest beach. Pebbly and sandy areas, great for a quick dip or evening walk.
  • Gudmindrup Strand: Beautiful wide sandy beach with shallow water, excellent for children. (15 min drive)
  • Høve Strand: Sandy beach surrounded by pine forest. (10 min drive)
  • Ordrup Næs: Beautiful nature reserve perfect for walking and finding quiet coastal spots.

Community Rules

Gyvelbakken 10 is part of Strandgaardens Grundejerforening. To maintain good relationships with our neighbors, please adhere to community standards:

  • Quiet Hours: Keep noise levels low outdoors after 22:00. The natural acoustics of the hills carry sound very far.
  • Outdoor Fires: Open bonfires are strictly regulated due to fire risk in the hilly terrain. Use only designated BBQ facilities.
  • Speed Limit: Drive at a crawling pace (max 20 km/h) on all gravel roads.
