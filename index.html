Driving & Navigation

Veddinge Bakker is known for its steep, winding roads. Please follow the posted speed limits. During the snowy season, note that only the route to Gyvelbakken via Ringbakken is plowed.

Public Transport

  • Train: Take the regional train from Copenhagen Central Station to Holbæk. In Holbæk, transfer to the local train (Lokaltog 510R) heading towards Nykøbing Sjælland. You can get off at either Asnæs Station or Fårevejle Station.
  • Bus: From Asnæs or Fårevejle station, you can take Bus 568 to Klintebakken (Strandgårdsvej), which takes about 32 min. and runs once an hour. The walk from the bus stop to the house takes about 10 min. Note that some of the hills are steep, and the bus only runs once an hour, so if you take the train, you should probably plan on getting a ride from someone between the train station and the house.

Parking

Proper parking is essential in Veddinge Bakker to ensure emergency vehicles and neighbors can always pass on the narrow roads.

  • Driveway Capacity: There is space for 2-3 cars to park in our driveway, marked 1 on the map.
  • Additional Parking: If you need additional parking space, you can use the area on the grass marked 2 on the map
  • Do Not Block the Road: Never leave cars parked out on the main road (Gyvelbakken).

Waste Sorting

Odsherred Kommune has strict sorting rules. Please separate garbage into the designated bins outside the property.

Under the kitchen sink, we put general waste (restaffald) in the front bin and food waste (madaffald) in the rear bin. Note: Odsherred Kommune does not require special bag types for food waste; just use the same ones as for general waste.

Espresso Machine

You are welcome to use the Sage Breville BES980 espresso machine.

  • Manual: If you are not familiar with this type of espresso machine, please consult the manual, which is available in the house manuals folder.
  • Water Tank: Refill the tank using bottled water rather than tap water. If you run out of bottled water, look for kildevand (uden brus) brands such as Aqua d'Or, Egekilde, or Denice.
  • Steamer: Remember to wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth after frothing milk.
  • Maintenance: If the machine indicates it needs descaling, a new filter, etc., please provide the necessary service using the supplies in the pantry.

Beaches

Odsherred offers some of the best coastline in Denmark.

  • Veddinge Strand: The closest beach, a 5 min. walk down the hill; entrance at the corner of Gyvelbakken and Lerbakken. Pebbly and sandy areas, great for a quick dip or evening walk.
  • Høve Strand: Sandy beach surrounded by pine forest. (10 min. drive)
  • Gudmindrup Strand: Beautiful wide sandy beach with shallow water, excellent for children. (20 min. drive)
