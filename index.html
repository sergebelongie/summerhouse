Property Overview

Welcome to our summer home in Veddinge Bakker! Nestled in the hills of Odsherred, the property offers a quiet space for relaxation, family stays, or focused small-group work sessions.

Space & Sleeping Layout

  • Bedrooms: Comfortable arrangements suitable for up to 6 adults.
  • Main Living Area: Large dining table suitable for shared meals or collaborative work sessions.
  • Outdoors: Elevated wooden deck with seating and views of the surrounding hilly terrain.

Work Session / Offsite Setup

If you are here with a team for a work session (e.g. Kalundborg regional projects), here are quick connectivity details:

  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Suitable for simultaneous video calls and work streams.
  • Workspaces: Main dining room table comfortably seats 6 with nearby power outlets. Quiet corners available for individual calls.
  • Parking: Space for 2-3 cars on the property driveway. Please do not block Gyvelbakken.

Heating & Climate

Heat Pump (Varmepumpe)

The primary heat source is the heat pump in the main room. Use the remote control on the wall to set temperature (recommended: 20°C - 22°C while staying).

Wood Stove (Brændeovn)

Firewood is located under the deck shelter. Please ensure the flue damper is open before lighting a fire.

Waste Sorting (Odsherred Kommune)

Odsherred Kommune has strict sorting rules. Please separate garbage into the designated bins outside:

  • Restaffald: General non-recyclable waste.
  • Madaffald: Food waste in green biodegradable bags.
  • Plast / Papir / Metal: Clean dry recyclables in the dual bin.
📄 View Waste Sorting Guide (PDF)

Strandgaardens Grundejerforening

Gyvelbakken 10 is part of Strandgaardens Grundejerforening. To maintain good relationships with our neighbours, please adhere to community standards:

  • Quiet Hours: Keep noise levels low outdoors after 22:00, especially given the natural acoustics of the hills.
  • Outdoor Fires: Open bonfires are strictly regulated due to fire risk in the hilly terrain. Use only designated BBQ facilities.
  • Garbage Bins: Ensure bin lids are securely fastened so wildlife cannot access waste.
